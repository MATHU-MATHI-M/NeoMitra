{{ _('Understanding Anemia During Pregnancy') }}

{{ _('What you need to know about this common pregnancy condition') }}

{{ _('Anemia during pregnancy occurs when you don\'t have enough red blood cells to carry oxygen to your tissues and to your baby. The most common cause is iron deficiency. During pregnancy, your body needs extra iron to make more blood to supply oxygen to your baby.') }}

{{ _('Common Symptoms of Anemia') }}
  • {{ _('Fatigue and weakness') }}
  • {{ _('Pale skin, lips, and nails') }}
  • {{ _('Dizziness or lightheadedness') }}
  • {{ _('Shortness of breath') }}
  • {{ _('Rapid heartbeat') }}
  • {{ _('Difficulty concentrating') }}

{{ _('Anemia in pregnancy is typically diagnosed when hemoglobin levels fall below 11 g/dL in the first and third trimesters, or below 10.5 g/dL in the second trimester.') }}

{{ _('Iron Supplementation Guidelines') }}
{{ _('When Diet Alone Is Not Enough') }}

{{ _('Even with a balanced diet rich in iron, pregnant women often need supplements to meet increased iron requirements.') }}

{{ _('Recommended Daily Intake') }}
  • {{ _('Non-pregnant women') }} 18 mg
  • {{ _('Pregnant women') }} 27 mg
  • {{ _('Women with anemia during pregnancy') }} 60-120 mg
{{ _('Tips for Taking Iron Supplements') }}
  • {{ _('Take iron with vitamin C to enhance absorption') }}
  • {{ _('Avoid taking with calcium supplements or milk') }}
  • {{ _('Take on an empty stomach if possible, but with food if it causes stomach upset') }}
{{ _('Important:') }}

{{ _('Always consult with your healthcare provider before starting any supplements. Too much iron can be harmful, and the right dosage depends on your individual needs.') }}

{{ _('Cooking Tips to Maximize Iron Absorption') }}
{{ _('Cook in Iron Pots') }}

{{ _('Cooking acidic foods like tomato sauce in cast iron cookware can increase the iron content of your meals.') }}

{{ _('Add Vitamin C') }}

{{ _('Squeeze lemon juice over iron-rich foods or include a vitamin C source with meals to enhance iron absorption.') }}

{{ _('Time Your Tea and Coffee') }}

{{ _('Avoid tea and coffee with meals as they can inhibit iron absorption. Wait at least an hour before or after meals.') }}

{{ _('Properly Prepare Legumes') }}

{{ _('Soak beans and lentils before cooking to reduce phytates that can inhibit iron absorption.') }}
